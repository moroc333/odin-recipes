Flan

Description

Flan is a simple yet delectable dessert of french origin. It has very little ingredients, yet it can be a little tricky in its preparation. Despite this though , it's silky texture and scrumptious flavor make up for all possible effort

ingredients

  • sugar (3/4 cup)
  • eggs (6 pieces)
  • condensed milk (1 can)
  • evaporated milk (1 can)
  • vanilla extract (1 tablespoon)

Steps

  1. Preheat the oven at 200°C.
  2. Put the flan mold over the burner at low heat and add the sugar.
  3. Stir the sugar constantly until it becomes a golden brown caramel.
  4. Move the mold around so the bottom gets evenly coated.
  5. Add the eggs, the condensed milk and the evaporated milk to the blender. Blend them together.
  6. Pour the mix inside the flan mold over the caramel. Cover it well with the flan mold lid or tin foil.
  7. Heat some water.
  8. Put your flan mold inside a bigger oven-safe container. Put them both inside the oven and pour hot water in the bigger container up to the middle of the mold flan height.
  9. Lower the heat at 180°C and let the flan cook for 50-60 min.
  10. Put your flan out of the oven and let it cool, preferably over night.
  11. Flan should be ready to take out of the mold.
  12. Enjoy!
