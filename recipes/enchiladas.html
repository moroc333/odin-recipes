Enchiladas

Ingredients

  • green tomatillo (1/ 2kg.)
  • garlic (2 cloves)
  • onion (1/2 piece)
  • serrano chilis (2-3 pieces-optional)
  • corn tortillas (1/2 kg.)
  • water (1 cup)
  • sour cream (optional)
  • grated cheese (optional)
  • oil (2 tablespoon)
  • salt (1/2 teaspoon)
  • shredded chicken (optional)

Steps

  1. In a medium pot, add the water and let it boil.
  2. Once boiling, add the tomatillo, garlic, onion and chilis until the tomatillos become pale green.
  3. Let the contents of the pot cool off a little and add them to the blender. Mix them thoroughtly.
  4. Get the pot back over the burner at medium heat. Once hot, add the oil.
  5. Carefully, but swiftly, pour the blended mix back into the pot. Let it boil.
  6. Remove the sauce from the heat
  7. Start picking up warm tortillas from the pile and dip them in the sauce (salsa). Put them on a plate and roll them up.
  8. Add a little more salsa on top.
  9. Garnish with shredded chicken, sour cream and grated cheese. (Optional, but suggested)
  10. Optionally, you can put the plate inside the oven or the grill to melt the cheese.
  11. Enchiladas are ready to serve.
  12. Enjoy!
