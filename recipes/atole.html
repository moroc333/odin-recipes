Atole

Ingredients

  • masa harina or cornsartch (1/3 cup)
  • warm water (3/4 cup)
  • milk(2 cups)
  • water (2 cups)
  • piloncillo or brown sugar (1/3 cup)
  • cinnamon (1 stick)
  • salt (1 pinch)
  • vanilla extract (1teaspoon)

Steps

  1. In a bowl, mix the masa harina in the warm water until completely dissolved.
  2. In a medium pot on medium heat pour the rest of the water, add the cinammon and let it simmer.
  3. Add milk until the mix simmers a little more.
  4. Once cinnamon has infused in the mix, remove the cinnamon stick.
  5. Pour the disolved masa into the pot.
  6. Stir slowly but constantly at low heat.
  7. Add the salt, vanilla and sugar while still stirring.
  8. Once the mix thickens,remove from heat
  9. The atole is ready to be served.
  10. Enjoy!
